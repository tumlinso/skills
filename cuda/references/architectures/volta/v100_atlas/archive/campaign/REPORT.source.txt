Nibble lookup has no observed complete-path crossover. Allocation remains outside the timed path. The E11 packed AND+POPC implementation has the lowest complete-path median at every tested universe; representation preparation dominates the tensor alternatives here.

Energy per reported work unit is 1.78360e-11 J and 1.66883e-11 J. These are read-only sensor estimates from sequential synthetic runs, not calibrated wall-plug measurements or a randomized policy comparison. The full resident-run event vectors remain available; the primary median/p95 uses only complete events within the stable interval. Sampling variation, sensor granularity, and thermal/order effects limit interpretation. Energy below sensor resolution remains unknown.

E20 accounts for all twelve directed pairs: eight permit public direct peer access and four do not. E21 records `set_mempolicy bind unavailable: Invalid argument` for node 1. The supported subcases have measurements and captures; unsupported subcases retain explicit `NOT_RUN` reasons. E04 and E27–E30 retain their original restricted hypotheses and were not run. Public neighboring tests do not validate those hypotheses.

Profiler replay supplies counters, while unprofiled timings supply performance samples. E31 concurrency and E33 steady-state behavior have separate diagnostic timelines; their replay limitations remain explicit. The copy-only E20/E21 timelines contain actual CUDA memcpy rows despite the installed wrapper's kernel-only negative classification. No machine setting was changed. Final read-only cleanup checks found all GPUs idle in Default compute mode with 300 W power limits and no surviving campaign MPS process.
