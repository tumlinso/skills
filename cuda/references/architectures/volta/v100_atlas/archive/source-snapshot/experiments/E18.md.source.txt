## Measured coverage (2026-10-06)

Eight correctness-valid GPU cases compare dependent pointer chains, independent peer loads, and peer-copy staging followed by a local chain at 16 KiB and 1 MiB working sets. The dependent-chain unit is p <- next[p], repeated for 4,000 or 32,000 steps; independent cases accumulate 4 or 32 indexed loads per thread. Result configurations use CUDA-visible ordinals 0/1, which the run lease and inventory bind to physical GPUs 0/2 (UUIDs GPU-21131915-1488-23af-38dd-1743ae1f5cc8 and GPU-d745da9c-7649-8334-41f1-483afa6f3206). At 1 MiB, local and remote dependent-chain kernel medians are 1.312 and 2.450 ms; staging preparation is 0.015 ms and the following local-chain median is 1.238 ms. At 16 KiB, local and remote chain medians are 0.168 and 0.311 ms. Staging and resident-kernel intervals are reported separately.

## Meaning through representation and execution

- **Supports:** Distinct costs for dependent remote access, independent peer loads, and explicit staging followed by local traversal on this allocation and physical pair.
- **Design implication (inference):** Staging can exchange an upfront transfer cost for a subsequent local access path; the choice depends on reuse and working set.
- **Does not establish:** Which cache serves accesses, requester-side L2 caching, coherent writable aliases, or a general peer-topology rule.
- **Original protocol gaps:** The run does not sweep cache forms, strides, concurrency, allocation alternatives, or owner-side traffic; it is not a cache-coherence proof.
