## Measured coverage (2026-10-06)

The matrix covers all 12 directed GPU pairs. Eight directed paths allow direct peer access and four are skipped because CUDA reports no direct access. The run records 4 MiB one-way and simultaneous two-way cases for supported pairs (16 measured cases plus four skips). One-way payload rates are 111.9–114.7 GB/s for physical pairs 0↔2 and 1↔3, and 5.16–5.17 GB/s for pairs 0↔1 and 2↔3. Bidirectional aggregate payload rates are reported separately from one-way rates. The copy timing is the complete selected transfer interval; no independent link counter was collected.

## Meaning through representation and execution

- **Supports:** Pair-specific payload-rate observations and CUDA's direct-access eligibility for the twelve tested directions.
- **Design implication (inference):** The faster and slower pair groups are consistent with the observed NVLink placement, which makes topology-aware pair selection a reasonable hypothesis to measure.
- **Does not establish:** Physical route for each payload, link saturation, multi-hop forwarding, or scientific graph-partition quality. Aggregate bidirectional rates cannot be compared to one-way payload rates.
- **Original protocol gaps:** Multiple-stream saturation and concurrent owner compute were not tested; the four ineligible directions have no direct-copy performance result.
