## Measured coverage (2026-10-06)

Six correctness-valid GPU cases cover 32 and 1,024 exact +1 completions using contended atomics, warp aggregation, and a 16-bin spread variant. Each reports 30 timing samples. At 32 updates, reported medians are 7.168 microseconds for contended atomics and warp aggregation, and 6.656 microseconds for spread; at 1,024, medians are 7.168 microseconds for all three. The 1,024-update p95 is 21.504 microseconds for contended atomics and 7.168 microseconds for the other two variants. The reported atomic transaction counts are implementation-based estimates: 32 versus one for the 32-update aggregated case, and 1,024 versus 32 for 1,024 updates. Nsight Systems is timeline/setup evidence and flagged allocator/launch contribution.

## Meaning through representation and execution

- **Supports:** The measured equality of update results and the estimated reduction in atomic operations for warp aggregation on these cases.
- **Design implication (inference):** Aggregating same-key updates can trade local coordination for fewer global atomic transactions when the exact increment semantics permit it.
- **Does not establish:** A measured hardware transaction count, a universal latency win, fairness, or results for other operations, widths, distributions, contention levels, or returned-value dependence.
- **Original protocol gaps:** The selected subset does not cover reservation and transition workloads, failed-CAS sweeps, or equal-semantics phased alternatives.
