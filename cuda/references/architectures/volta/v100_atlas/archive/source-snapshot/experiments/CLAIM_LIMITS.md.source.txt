## Reading the boundaries

Statuses describe this run, not protocol completion: `GPU_RUN` covers some checked cases; `CPU_ONLY` is host-side; `NOT_RUN` leaves a hypothesis untested. E36 compiled-only means metadata inspection. Absence is not impossibility.
